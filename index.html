The Artist

Bartertown is Bobby Fuller — a self-taught artist working around Lynchburg and Bedford, Virginia. He grew up poor in the Norfolk and Virginia Beach inner city, building his own toys out of what other people threw away, and he never stopped: gears become owls, book pages become pack horses, barn doors become canvases, and city walls become stories.

Nothing is bought. Every material is found, donated, or traded — 100% salvage. "Even though they are made from trash, I treat them like they are glass," he told Lynchburg Living. Best in Show winner at the Academy Center of the Arts juried exhibition, with wheat-paste figures and murals scattered across downtown walls. Every piece is one of one.

— Bartertown Bobby
