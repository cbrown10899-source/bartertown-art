The Artist

Bartertown Art is one man, a pile of salvage, and no off switch. The work comes off canvas, barn doors, butcher paper, boxcars, and whatever the scrapyard gives up — gears become owls, book pages become pack horses, and city walls become stories.

Paintings, murals, found-object sculpture, commissions — if it can hold paint or a weld, it's fair game. Every piece is one of one.

— this is art.
